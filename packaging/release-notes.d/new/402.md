- Read a mailbox whose server offers POP3 alone: Add Account › Other (POP) downloads the account's Inbox by UIDL (RFC 1939), over TLS or STARTTLS, and sends through the account's own SMTP server. Mail fetched stays on the server unless you say otherwise (a switch in its settings), and what the server no longer has goes from here too ([#402](https://github.com/GITHUB_REPOSITORY/issues/402)).
